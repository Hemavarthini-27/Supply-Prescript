# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Project Title

SupplyPrescript - Closed Loop Prescriptive Analytics for Supply Chain Management

---

## 1. Problem Statement

Many companies experience shipment delays due to traffic, weather, supplier issues, transportation mode, and warehouse constraints.

Current logistics systems can identify delays after they occur, but they often do not recommend the best corrective action.

This project aims to predict shipment delays and recommend the best business decision before delays become critical.

---

## 2. Business Objectives

- Predict shipment delays.
- Recommend the optimal transportation strategy.
- Reduce logistics cost.
- Improve on-time delivery.
- Help logistics managers make better decisions.

---

## 3. Project Scope

### In Scope
- Predict shipment delays using Machine Learning.
- Recommend the best logistics action.
- Build an interactive dashboard.
- Analyze shipment performance.
- Generate business insights.

### Out of Scope
- Real-time GPS tracking.
- Automatic vehicle routing.
- Live weather API integration.

## 4. Stakeholders

- Logistics Manager
- Warehouse Manager
- Supply Chain Analyst
- Operations Team

---

## 5. Expected Inputs

- Shipment ID
- Order Date
- Delivery Date
- Origin
- Destination
- Transport Mode
- Shipment Cost
- Weather Condition
- Distance
- Product Category
- Supplier
- Warehouse

---

## 6. Expected Outputs

- Delay Prediction
- Delay Probability
- Recommended Action
- Estimated Cost
- Expected Delivery Time

---

## 7. Success Metrics

- Prediction Accuracy
- Reduced Delivery Delay
- Reduced Transportation Cost
- Better Resource Utilization

---

## 8. Constraints

- Limited historical data
- Missing values
- Seasonal demand
- Weather uncertainty

---

## 9. Assumptions

- Historical data represents future trends.
- Weather data is available.
- Transportation cost is known.
- Warehouse capacity is available.
